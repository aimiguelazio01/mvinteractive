## Brand & Style
The **Apple Harmonics** email design system is built upon the synthesis of organic matter and digital technology. The style is **premium dark**, characterized by sleek, dark gray textures, responsive table architecture, and high-fidelity product imagery. It mirrors the interactive landing page's main features: tactile response, sonic signals, and volumetric physics.

## Colors
The color palette represents an orchard at dusk, where deep shadowed backgrounds meet bright, contrasting elements:
- **Orchard Shadows (Dark `#131313`)**: The primary canvas background, creating a high-end cinematic space.
- **Apple Core White (`#e5e2e1`)**: High-readability font color for main body content.
- **Deep Apple Red (`#991b1b`)**: Used as the primary accent color for active segments and structural headers.
- **Orchard Green (`#b8cda8`)**: Represents organic growth and foliage, serving as secondary highlighting.
- **Copper Orange (`#ffb77b`)**: Highlights interactive prompts, mimicking active copper sensors.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm balances high-tech precision with human-scale readability:
- **Headlines (Orbitron / Oswald)**: Used in all capitals to convey structural rigidity and digital feedback.
- **Body & Information (Inter)**: Delivers organic descriptions, instructions, and notes clearly.
- **Labels (Space Grotesk)**: Styled in small, tracked-out capital letters to guide reading flow.

## Spacing & Responsive Structure
The template employs a centered 600px width with structured margin boundaries:
- **Fluid Layout**: Designed with a single outer wrapper scaling down on mobile devices.
- **Stacked Columns**: Side-by-side cells collapse into single-column elements under screen widths of 600px using standard `@media` stylesheets.
